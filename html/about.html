Why a study planner?

A study planner helps you organize your study time effectively, set goals, and track your progress. It ensures you make the most of your study sessions and stay on top of deadlines.

The Study Planner is a simple web application designed to help students organize their academic tasks and manage deadlines more effectively. Users can add study tasks, view their schedule, and keep track of important dates in one convenient place. The application demonstrates the use of HTML for structure, CSS for responsive design, and JavaScript for dynamic interaction and local storage, ensuring tasks remain saved between sessions. Its goal is to promote better planning habits while showcasing core web development concepts.
